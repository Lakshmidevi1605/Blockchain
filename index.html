The ledger

From prescription to pickup, in four recorded steps

01

Doctor prescribes

The doctor issues a prescription through their dashboard, with dosage, duration, and notes.

02

Hashed to chain

The record is hashed and written to the blockchain, generating a unique, tamper-evident ID.

03

Patient reviews

The patient views their prescription and QR code from their own dashboard, anytime.

04

Pharmacy verifies

The pharmacy scans the QR code, checks it against the chain, and dispenses safely.
